Weeknight Garlic Pasta

By Dana Ruiz · Updated September 2026 · Serves 4 · 20 min

When there is nothing in the house but pasta and garlic, this is dinner. It takes twenty minutes and one pot.

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 6 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • Chili flakes, parsley, salt

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water.
  2. Warm the garlic in the oil on low heat until golden.
  3. Toss the pasta with the oil, chili, and parsley.
