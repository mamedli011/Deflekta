One-Bowl Banana Bread

By Dana Ruiz · Updated September 2026 · Makes 1 loaf · 1 hr 5 min

The recipe that finally used up every brown banana on my counter. No mixer, one bowl, and it stays moist for days.

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp baking soda, pinch of salt
  • 1 1/2 cups flour

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 175 °C and butter a loaf pan.
  2. Mash the bananas in a bowl, then stir in the butter.
  3. Mix in the sugar, egg, baking soda, and salt.
  4. Fold in the flour until just combined.
  5. Bake for 55 to 60 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
