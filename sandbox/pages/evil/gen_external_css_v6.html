Creamy Tomato Soup

By Dana Ruiz · Updated September 2026 · Serves 4 · 35 min

Grilled cheese weather deserves a proper tomato soup. This one uses canned tomatoes and is ready in half an hour.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 cans (800 g) whole tomatoes
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • 1/2 cup cream, fresh basil

Steps

  1. Soften the onion in the oil for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for one minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes and stock and simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Blend until smooth, then stir in the cream.
  5. Season and serve with torn basil.
