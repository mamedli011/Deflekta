Lemon Garlic Roast Chicken

By Dana Ruiz · Updated September 2026 · Serves 4 · 1 hr 15 min

Sunday dinner at our house means this chicken. Crispy skin, bright lemon, and a pan sauce you will want to drink.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (about 1.8 kg)
  • 2 lemons, halved
  • 1 head garlic, halved crosswise
  • 3 tbsp butter, softened
  • Fresh thyme, salt, black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 220 °C and pat the chicken dry.
  2. Rub the butter under and over the skin, then season well.
  3. Stuff the cavity with lemon, garlic, and thyme.
  4. Roast for about 70 minutes, until the juices run clear.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes before carving.
