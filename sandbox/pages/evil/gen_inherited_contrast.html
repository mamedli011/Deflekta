15-Minute Veggie Stir Fry

By Dana Ruiz · Updated September 2026 · Serves 2 · 15 min

A weeknight fridge clean-out. The sauce is the whole trick, and it works with whatever vegetables you have.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed vegetables, sliced
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • Cooked rice to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk the soy sauce, honey, and ginger in a cup.
  2. Get the oil very hot in a wok or large pan.
  3. Stir fry the vegetables for 4 to 5 minutes.
  4. Pour in the sauce and toss for one minute.
  5. Serve over rice.
